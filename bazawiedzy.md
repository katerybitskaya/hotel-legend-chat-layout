# GENERAL DESCRIPTION
Hotel Legend is a cozy sanctuary of refinement in the heart of the Silesian Beskids, where historical grandeur meets modern luxury. Located in the cultural heart of Bielsko-Biała, Hotel Legend is a tribute to the rich Austro-Hungarian heritage of the region. Our four-star residence occupies a carefully restored building from the early 20th century, offering 62 individually designed rooms and suites.

From the moment you enter, the warm glow of brass elements, hand-laid stone floors, and a carefully selected collection of local art signal an experience completely different from the ordinary. Our dedicated team of 80 hospitality professionals lives by one belief: every guest deserves to feel like the hero of their own legend.
