Our Story

A Legacy of
Refined Hospitality

Nestled in the cultural heart of Bielsko-Biała, Hotel Legend stands as a tribute to the region's rich Austro-Hungarian heritage. Our four-star residence occupies a meticulously restored early-20th-century building, offering 62 individually designed rooms and suites.

From the moment you enter, the warm glow of brass fixtures, hand-laid stone floors, and a curated collection of local artwork signal an experience entirely apart from the ordinary. Our dedicated team of 80 hospitality professionals lives one conviction: that every guest deserves to feel like the protagonist of their own legend.

In the Heart of the City

Hotel Legend is ideally positioned in the historic centre of Bielsko-Biała — a city known for its magnificent Austro-Hungarian architecture, vibrant arts scene and the gateway to the Silesian Beskids mountain range.

Getting Here

We are easily reachable by car via the A1 motorway (exit Bielsko-Biała Centre), by train to Bielsko-Biała Central Station (12 min walk), and by air via Katowice International Airport (65 km, approx. 55 min by car). Private airport transfers available on request.

Nearby Attractions

  • Bielsko-Biała Old Town Market 3 min walk
  • Sułkowski Castle 5 min walk
  • Żywiec Brewery Museum 30 min drive
  • Szczyrk Ski Resort 25 min drive
  • Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial 55 min drive
  • Katowice City Centre 60 min drive
